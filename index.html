How It Works

01 / QUESTIONNAIRE

Take the 3-minute survey

Sport, position, weekly training frequency and duration, season phase, goal (build muscle / maintain / manage weight / build endurance), dietary restrictions and allergens.

02 / MATCHING

We match it to the literature

Your data becomes a structured query, checked against ISSN position stands, the ACSM/AND/DC joint statement, and IOC consensus statements — all reviewed by a sports-science advisor.

03 / YOUR PLAN

Get your personalized plan

Daily macro targets (grams and g/kg), pre/during/post-training fueling windows, and local food examples — every recommendation cites its source and can be expanded.

TIMING · FUELING WINDOW

Within 30–60 minutes after training, aim for roughly 20–40g of protein to kick-start muscle repair and adaptation.CONFIDENCE: HIGH

Thomas, D.T. et al. Position of the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics, Dietitians of Canada, and ACSM: Nutrition and Athletic Performance. J Acad Nutr Diet, 2016.
MACROS · SOCCER, IN-SEASON

On training days, target 6–10 g/kg body mass of carbohydrate to maintain muscle glycogen stores and support repeated sprint performance.CONFIDENCE: HIGH

Practical nutritional recovery strategies for elite soccer players when limited time separates repeated matches. Br J Sports Med, 2017.
SAFETY · FENCING WEIGHT MANAGEMENT

Weight-class management should follow a gradual approach, capped at 0.5% of body weight lost per week. The system flags rapid-cut intent and notifies the coach dashboard.CONFIDENCE: MODERATE

Lomazzi, M. Nutrition for European Elite Fencers: A Practical Tool for Coaches and Athletes. Nutrients, 2024, 16(8), 1104.

Safety rules
override
every output
  • Daily calories never drop below the estimated BMR safety floor
  • Weekly weight change capped at 0.5% of body weight for minors
  • Fencing weight management is conservative-only; rapid-cut intent triggers a coach alert
  • No supplement recommendations at all in the MVP — food only
  • Red-flag signals (e.g. very low BMI plus a weight-loss goal) surface support resources instead of a weight-loss plan
  • Minors must complete parental/guardian consent before data collection is enabled
